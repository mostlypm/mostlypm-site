I'm Gayatri, a product manager with 10+ years in enterprise software.

Most of my recent work is with teams putting AI into serious workflows: financial services, document review, data-heavy operations. People there need to trust the output before they act on it. On these projects I've done the whole range: turning a BRD and milestone plans into something buildable, prototyping the screens myself, auditing what the system actually stores against what the spec says, and building eval harnesses so "it looks fine" becomes a number anyone on the team can check.

I work with AI coding agents every day, and that changes what a PM can deliver. I don't hand over a document and wait. I bring a working prototype, a test set and a baseline report.

Through mostlypm, I offer this as focused engagements: a few weeks, a clear deliverable, and a team that can keep using it after I leave. Part-time and remote, from India (IST).
